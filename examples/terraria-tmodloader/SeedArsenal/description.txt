Weapons, enemies and a boss that aren't in vanilla Terraria, with sprites generated with FixedSeed (FLUX.1 dev).

Weapons: Homing Missile Launcher, Tactical Nuke, Tesla Rifle, Singularity Launcher, Orbital Strike.
Enemies: Scrap Drone, Neon Slime, Mech Walker (surface). Boss: Drone Mothership.

Type /arsenal in chat for the weapons (or craft them at an anvil) and /mothership to summon the boss.
Explosions dig into the world: try it in a spare world.
